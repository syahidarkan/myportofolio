/ About Me

I turn business problems into working systems.

Developer. Product Designer. AI Integrator. Founder.

I study Information Systems at Fasilkom UI. Outside class, I run PandaTech, a software studio I founded, and build digital products across multiple verticals: otwptn for university admissions consulting, splitbill for group billing, and web systems for events, government agencies, and businesses. Most of what I build starts from a business problem that needs a digital solution.

At UI I got involved early: UI/UX staff at Open House Fasilkom, then Head of UI/UX Division at GDGoC UI. I also spent two months as a UI/UX intern in the IT division at ActionPay, a fintech startup in Jakarta. On the organizational side, I contribute to BEM UI's digital team and BEM Fasilkom's KASTRAT department, and currently lead the UI/UX division for Open House Fasilkom UI 2026.

What sets my work apart is how I use AI. Not just as a tool to write code faster, but to build systems where AI handles parts of the business process itself. I integrate LLMs and automation into products I build so they do more with less human intervention. The result is software that works harder than what most junior developers ship.
